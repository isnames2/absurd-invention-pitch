## The pitch

"The Polite Smoke Alarm clears its throat and whispers 'ahem, the toast' instead of screaming at 3 a.m. every time you burn toast."

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, pure standard library) writes the pitch to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. No network, deterministic output.
